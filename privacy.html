Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy & Culinary Data Stewardship

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

At CloveStewPine (accessible via https://clovestewpine.com), the protection, digital sovereignty, and privacy of our visitors, guild members, and culinary researchers represent foundational commitments. This comprehensive Privacy Policy document outlines the categories of data we receive, collect, record, process, and safeguard, as well as the mechanisms through which you can exercise your legal data privacy rights under applicable data protection frameworks, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Data Collection Categories and Technical Telemetry

We believe in radical data minimization. We collect only the information strictly necessary to provide our culinary monographs, interactive calculators, and seasonal culinary dispatches:

  • Directly Disclosed User Data: When you subscribe to our Hearthside Dispatch, fill out contact inquiries, register for workshops, or submit correspondence, we record your full name, email address, inquiry classification, and the textual content of your message.
  • Automated Telemetry & Measurement: When accessing our digital publications, our infrastructure utilizes Google Analytics (Tag ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) to collect anonymized usage logs. This telemetry includes browser user-agent strings, device operating systems, screen resolutions, referring and exit pages, timestamps, approximate regional geolocation (city/country level), and interaction durations across specific recipe monographs. All IP addresses are programmatically anonymized at the point of ingestion prior to persistent storage.
  • Client-Side Local Storage: Our platform employs HTML5 localStorage to store non-identifying operational preferences, such as your active theme selection (Light Mode versus Dark Mode) and cookie consent status. LocalStorage entries remain exclusively on your local device and are never transmitted across network calls.

2. Lawful Grounds for Processing Personal Information

In accordance with Article 6 of the GDPR, CloveStewPine processes personal records under the following legal bases:

  • User Consent: Clear, affirmative, and unambiguous consent granted by you when opting into our newsletter dispatches, downloading culinary PDFs, or accepting analytics tracking mechanisms.
  • Performance of a Contract: Processing necessary to fulfill educational workshop registrations, digital guild access, or direct customer communication.
  • Legitimate Interests: Monitoring website performance, optimizing layout responsiveness across mobile and desktop devices, diagnosing infrastructure faults, and defending against automated bot attacks or malicious exploits.
  • Legal Compliance: Retaining necessary transaction and communication records to satisfy statutory tax, accounting, or regulatory requirements.

3. Third-Party Integrations and Data Processors

CloveStewPine never sells, rents, monetizes, trades, or commercializes personal reader records to data brokers, advertising networks, or third-party marketing entities. Information is processed exclusively through trusted, SOC-2 certified cloud infrastructure providers who operate under rigorous Data Processing Agreements (DPAs):

  • Google Analytics: For aggregate statistical telemetry and readership volume tracking.
  • Cloudflare / CDN Edge Services: For DDoS protection, web traffic routing, and rapid global asset distribution via encrypted TLS 1.3 tunnels.
  • Transactional Email Services: For reliable delivery of requested newsletter issues and guild dispatches, equipped with single-click automated unsubscribe headers.

4. International Data Transfers & Storage Security

CloveStewPine operates secure server clusters located in North America and Western Europe. Where personal records are transferred across international borders, we implement European Commission-approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and enforce end-to-end cryptographic encryption both in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).

5. Data Retention Schedules

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes delineated in this policy:

  • Newsletter subscription records are retained until you explicitly unsubscribe or your email address bounces repeatedly.
  • Contact form dispatches and editorial correspondence are archived for twenty-four (24) months for reference before automated secure deletion.
  • Anonymized Google Analytics telemetry is retained on a rolling 14-month retention window before automated purging.

6. Your Comprehensive Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you are entitled to exercise comprehensive data rights:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request a complete export of all personal data held concerning your account in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Rectification: You may demand correction of any inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the permanent deletion of your personal records from our active databases and backup systems.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You may object at any time to the processing of your data for direct marketing or analytics purposes.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please contact our Data Protection Office at privacy@clovestewpine.com. We respond to all verified requests within thirty (30) calendar days without charge.
