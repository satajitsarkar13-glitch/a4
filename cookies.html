Cookie Policy & Local Storage Management

Effective Date: January 1, | Last Updated:

This Cookie Policy explains how CloveStewPine uses cookies, local browser storage, and related technologies to provide a seamless, secure, and personalized reading experience.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small alphanumeric text files placed on your device by your web browser. localStorage is a modern HTML5 storage standard that enables websites to save user interface preferences locally on your computer without transmitting data across network requests.

2. Classification of Storage Mechanisms Used

Name / Key Type Lifespan Purpose
clove_theme localStorage Persistent Remembers your choice between Light and Dark visual themes.
clove_cookie_choice localStorage Persistent Stores your cookie banner consent decision (accepted/essential).
_ga, _ga_* Cookie (Google Analytics) 2 Years Anonymized page readership and telemetry measurement.

3. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time by clearing your browser cache or clicking "Essential Only" on our consent banner. Additionally, all major web browsers provide settings to block third-party cookies or alert you when cookies are sent.
